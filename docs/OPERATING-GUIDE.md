# Operating guide

## For the cashier

### Opening the shop
1. Sign in on the shop phone or tablet.
2. **Shift** → count the cash in the till → type it as the **opening float** → tick the checklist → **Open shift**.

### Selling
1. **Sell** → tap the products (tap again for more, use − / + in the order).
2. Choose **Cash**, **Card** or **EFT**.
   - **Cash:** type what the customer gave, or tap *Exact*, *R50*, *R100* or *R200*. The app shows the change.
   - **Card:** take the payment on the card machine first. You can type the slip number.
   - **EFT:** only hand over the food when the payment is confirmed.
3. **Complete sale**. Then tap **Print receipt** or **WhatsApp receipt** if the customer wants one.
4. **No internet (load-shedding, no data)?** Keep selling. Each sale is saved on the phone, and an orange **Offline** label shows how many are waiting. They send by themselves when the internet is back, and are never recorded twice. Don't clear the browser or sign out until they've sent. You can't close the shift until they're through.
4. **Discounts** need a reason, and you can only give up to the limit the owner set.

### The order queue
Every paid order appears on **Orders** in the order customers paid ("Order 1", "Order 2"…), with any note for the kitchen (e.g. *no atchar*).
1. Tap **Start preparing** when you begin the kota.
2. Tap **✓ Ready** when it's packed. It moves to *Ready to collect*.
3. Tap **✓ Collected** when the customer takes it.

Orders turn orange after 10 minutes and red after 20, so nobody waits too long. Tapped the wrong button? Use **Undo**. The Orders tab shows how many orders are waiting, and the queue keeps working with no internet.

### Mistakes and refunds
- You can't delete or change a sale. Go to **Sales** → **Refund** → give the reason. The owner approves it. Only hand money back once it's approved.

### Spending money
- **Bought stock** (bread, polony, chips, drinks): **Stock › Delivery** → add each item, quantity and cost. Enter how much cash came from the till, and photograph the slip.
- **Other expenses** (gas, electricity, transport, cleaning): **Expenses** → amount, category, description, photograph the receipt → *Submit for approval*. Tick *Paid with cash from the till* if you used till cash.
- **Cash taken out** (for example, the owner collects cash): **Shift › Cash paid out**.

### Food that wasn't sold
**Stock › Wastage & meals** → choose *Wastage* (burnt or dropped), *Staff meal* or *Free item*, then the item, the quantity and the reason.

### Closing the shop
1. **Shift › Cash up & close**.
2. Count the notes and coins into the boxes. The total adds up for you. You won't see what the system expects. That's normal.
3. Type the **card machine batch total** from the end-of-day slip.
4. Count the stock that's left (bread, Russians, polony, cheese, drinks…). Items marked **must count** have to be counted or the shift won't close.
5. Tick the closing checklist → **Submit**.
6. If the cash doesn't match, recount first. If it still doesn't match, explain honestly what happened.
7. Only now can you sign out. The app won't let a cashier sign out while their shift is open.

## For the owner

### Every day (5 minutes on your phone)
- **Dashboard**: today's revenue, gross and operating profit, cash/card/EFT, the live till balance, low stock, best sellers and staff actions.
- **Approvals**: refunds, expenses (check the receipt photo) and stock corrections. Rejecting needs a note.
- **Cash-ups**: expected vs counted for each shift, card machine vs recorded card sales, explanations, checklists and stock count variances.
- **Sales**: tick card and EFT sales as *Verified* once they show on your bank statement or merchant portal. **⬇ CSV** downloads the day's sales.
- The dashboard compares sales with the same day last week (▲/▼).

### Ordering stock
**Stock › Levels** shows a **Reorder list** when items run low, grouped by supplier. Tap **WhatsApp order** to send the order to the supplier (their phone number comes from Ingredients › Suppliers), or **Copy** it.

### Weekly / monthly
- **Reports**: choose Week, Month or any dates. You get the profit statement, payment methods, daily chart, best sellers, expenses by category, cash differences and stock losses. **CSV** downloads it for your bookkeeper. **Print / PDF** saves it.
- Check **Ingredients** costs against supplier prices and update recipes, so profit estimates stay accurate.
- Review the **Activity log** for unusual discounts, refunds, payouts or corrections.

### Warning signs to look into
| Sign | Where |
|---|---|
| Cash short several days in a row | Cash-ups, then the CCTV footage at closing time |
| Many refunds or discounts by one person | Activity log, Sales |
| High wastage or stock count losses (especially Russians and cheese) | Reports › Stock losses |
| Long gaps with no sales while open | Alerts (*No sales for…*) |
| Shift not closed or shop not opened | Alerts |
| Expenses without receipts | Approvals |
| Many **offline** sales, or an *offline sale total changed* alert | Sales (orange *Offline* badge shows when it was really sold) |
| Missing stock on the same item again and again | Ingredients → tick **Must be counted at every close** |

### More than one shop
**My shops** (or tap the shop name at the top) lists your kota houses. **Open another kota house** creates a new, empty shop with its own menu, stock, cash-ups, staff and reports. **Switch** changes which shop this device is working in; each phone remembers its own choice. Cashiers belong to one shop and never see the others. Add each branch's cashier from **Staff** while you're switched to that branch.

### Staff changes
**Staff** → *Deactivate* stops a person immediately on every device. Their history stays. Use *New password* if a password leaks.

### Privacy
The app does not track anyone's location. CCTV is handled by your camera system (see CCTV.md). Tell staff what is recorded, as POPIA requires.
